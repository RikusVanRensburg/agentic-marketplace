# `CONTEXT.md` format

`CONTEXT.md` is the per-topic glossary. It lives at `<project-root>/docs/grilled/<topic-slug>/CONTEXT.md` and contains the resolved vocabulary for that topic only.

## Template

```markdown
# {Topic name}

{One or two sentence description of what this topic covers and why it was grilled.}

## Language

**{Term}**:
{One or two sentence definition — what it IS, not what it does.}
_Avoid_: {comma-separated aliases this term displaces}

**{Next term}**:
{...}

## Flagged ambiguities

- **{term}** — {short description of why ambiguous and what's pending}
```

## Three rules

1. **Glossary-only.** No implementation notes, no rationale, no scratchpad. If it explains *why* a choice was made, it belongs in an ADR, not here.
2. **Opinionated — one canonical term per concept.** Pick the term, write the definition once, list every alias that should *not* be used in `_Avoid_`. The point of grilling is to displace ambiguous synonyms.
3. **Tight definitions.** 1–2 sentences. Define what the term *is*, not what it does or how it's used. Behavior belongs in the surrounding code, not the glossary.

Cross-topic references go inline: `"…same as 'payment-flow.Customer'"` or in the `_Avoid_` line where relevant.

## When a term has no aliases to displace

Omit the `_Avoid_` line entirely. Do **not** write `_Avoid_: (none)`, `_Avoid_: —`, or an empty `_Avoid_:`. Absence is the convention.

```markdown
**IdempotencyKey**:
A SHA-256 of (customer_id, amount, ISO date) used to dedupe payment-retry attempts within a calendar day. Generated by the caller, not the server.
```

No `_Avoid_` line — there are no common synonyms to displace.

## When a term is canonized differently in another topic — override-with-note

When the cross-topic scan finds a meaning conflict and the user chooses **Override-with-note** (see [convention-detection.md](convention-detection.md)), the new entry must point at the other topic so future readers know the divergence is deliberate, not drift. Format: prefix the `_Avoid_` line with `see <other-topic>.<Term>` followed by the displaced aliases.

```markdown
**Customer**:
A party attempting checkout, including unauthenticated guests with only an email address. Distinct from the canonical Customer in the payment-retry-logic topic, which requires a placed Order.
_Avoid_: see payment-retry-logic.Customer; Buyer, Account, Shopper
```

The first `_Avoid_` token is the cross-reference; aliases follow after the semicolon. If the override has *no* aliases beyond the cross-reference, the `_Avoid_` line still appears with just the `see <other-topic>.<Term>` token:

```markdown
**Order**:
A subscription billing cycle, not a one-off purchase. Specific to the recurring-billing topic.
_Avoid_: see ecommerce-checkout.Order
```

## Worked example — e-commerce ordering

```markdown
# Order cancellation

Vocabulary for the cancel-and-refund flow on placed orders. Grilled to nail down who can cancel what and when.

## Language

**Order**:
A confirmed purchase intent containing one or more LineItems, owned by exactly one Customer.
_Avoid_: cart, basket, transaction

**LineItem**:
A single (product, quantity, price) row inside an Order. Independently cancellable when the Order is in Partial state.
_Avoid_: order line, product line, SKU entry

**Cancellation**:
The act of voiding an Order or one of its LineItems before fulfillment completes. A Cancellation is itself an audited event with an actor and a reason.
_Avoid_: refund, void, abort

**Refund**:
The monetary movement that follows a Cancellation when payment has already been captured. A Cancellation may exist without a Refund (uncaptured payment); a Refund never exists without a Cancellation.
_Avoid_: chargeback, return, reversal

## Flagged ambiguities

- **partial-fulfillment** — unclear whether a Cancellation issued after one LineItem ships should produce one Refund per remaining item or one aggregate Refund. Pending product decision.
```

## Worked example — document management

```markdown
# Document versioning

Vocabulary for how Documents are revised, branched, and finalized in the editor.

## Language

**Document**:
The top-level container identified by a stable DocumentId. All Revisions of the same content share one DocumentId.
_Avoid_: file, page, asset

**Revision**:
An immutable snapshot of a Document's body at a point in time. Revisions form a linear history per Document; branches are modeled as separate Documents.
_Avoid_: version, draft, edit

**Draft**:
The mutable working copy attached to a Document. Promoting a Draft creates a new Revision and clears the Draft. A Document has at most one Draft at a time.
_Avoid_: working copy, in-progress version, scratch

**Publication**:
The act of marking a specific Revision as the publicly visible one for its Document. Independent of Revision creation — old Revisions can be re-Published.
_Avoid_: release, go-live, commit

## Flagged ambiguities

- **branching** — repo currently models branches as separate Documents with a `forkedFrom` reference. User is undecided whether to canonize that or introduce a first-class Branch term. Re-grill when product decides.
```
